About Rehobot Choir

Discover our history, mission, and the core values that drive our worship.

Our History

Rehobot Choir was founded with a divine vision to bring believers together through the power of sacred music. Over the years, we have grown from a small group of passionate singers into a vibrant community dedicated to leading congregations into deeper worship and spiritual reflection.

Our Mission

To spread the gospel of Jesus Christ through uplifting choral music, inspire spiritual growth in our listeners, and foster a unified environment of heartfelt worship.

Our Vision

To be a leading instrument of praise and transformation, reaching communities far and wide with the message of hope, faith, and eternal love.

Our Core Values

  • Faith: Keeping Christ at the center of everything we sing and do.
  • Unity: Harmonizing our voices and our hearts as one family.
  • Excellence: Dedicating our best time, talent, and energy to our craft.
  • Service: Serving our church and broader community with humility and love.
